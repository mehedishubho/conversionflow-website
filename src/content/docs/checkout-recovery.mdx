# Checkout Recovery

Checkout recovery turns incomplete order activity into actionable sales follow-up. Instead of waiting for a completed payment, ConversionFlow treats checkout intent as operational data.

## What to Capture

- Customer name and phone
- Product and cart context
- Checkout step reached
- Payment method intent
- Campaign or tracking source when available

## How Teams Use It

Sales and support teams can follow up quickly, answer objections, request booking money, or recover orders that failed because of payment friction.
