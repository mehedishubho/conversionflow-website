# ConversionFlow Platform Editions

ConversionFlow is a multi-platform commerce operations ecosystem. The edition you choose depends on who owns implementation and how much control your team needs over tracking, courier workflows, partial payments, and analytics.

## WooCommerce Edition

Choose this edition when the store runs on WordPress and the operator wants the fastest route to Meta CAPI, courier automation, COD protection, partial payments, and checkout recovery.

## Laravel Edition

Choose this edition for custom Laravel commerce stacks that need API-ready event workflows, operational customization, and developer-controlled integrations.

## Next.js / MERN Edition

Choose this edition for headless commerce teams that need server-side event architecture, React-friendly implementation patterns, and scalable analytics infrastructure.

## Upgrade Path

Many teams start with WooCommerce and later move into Laravel or headless commerce. ConversionFlow keeps the operating model consistent: reliable ad signals, courier intelligence, fraud visibility, and better delivery outcomes.
