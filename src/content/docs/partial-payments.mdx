# Partial Payments and COD Booking Money

Partial payment workflows help COD-heavy stores confirm buyer commitment before inventory is dispatched. ConversionFlow positions booking money as an operational control, not just a payment feature.

## Recommended Flow

1. Identify high-risk COD orders using customer history, delivery patterns, and order value.
2. Request booking money or an advance payment before courier booking.
3. Verify bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or bank transfer payment.
4. Continue fulfillment once the order has enough commitment.

## Operational Benefit

Advance payment reduces fake dispatches, protects courier cost, and gives support teams a clearer reason to prioritize serious buyers.
