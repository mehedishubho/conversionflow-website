# Customer Courier History

Customer courier history gives operations teams context before accepting or dispatching COD orders. A buyer with repeated returns or failed deliveries should be handled differently from a buyer with successful delivery history.

## Signals to Review

- Delivered orders
- Returned orders
- Cancelled orders
- Failed delivery attempts
- Courier partner performance by customer region

## Why It Matters

Delivery intelligence helps stores reduce avoidable courier cost, identify risky orders, and protect campaign profitability after the order is placed.
